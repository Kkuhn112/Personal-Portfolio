Robotics • Controls • Fabrication

PAROL6 Robotic Arm

Problem: Build a functional desktop robotic arm platform that could be assembled, wired, calibrated, and controlled reliably enough for repeatable motion testing.

Constraints: Printed mechanical parts, compact wiring paths, joint alignment, stepper motor behavior, calibration accuracy, and safe benchtop operation.

Approach: Assembled the PAROL6 arm, routed wiring through moving joints, configured controller software, checked each axis individually, and iterated on mechanical fit and cable management.

Result: Produced a working robotics platform for learning six-axis motion, controller setup, system troubleshooting, and mechanical-electrical integration.

Tools: 3D printing, mechanical assembly, wiring, motion control software, calibration, troubleshooting

Programming • 3D Design • Prototyping

Custom Macro Pad

Problem: Create a compact programmable input device that improves repeated desktop workflows without taking much desk space.

Constraints: Switch spacing, comfortable key layout, USB port access, enclosure printability, internal clearance, and reliable key mapping.

Approach: Designed the enclosure in CAD, 3D printed the housing, assembled the keys and electronics, and wrote the macro logic for custom shortcuts.

Result: Built a functional eight-key macro pad combining mechanical packaging, additive manufacturing, and programming into a practical tool.

Tools: CAD, FDM printing, keyboard switches, embedded-style programming, iterative fit checks

Research • Composites • Python

Continuous Fiber 3D Printing Toolpath System

Problem: Improve printed composite behavior by controlling continuous fiber placement relative to expected crack growth and loading direction.

Constraints: Fiber bending limits, nozzle path continuity, G-code compatibility, specimen geometry, and repeatable test conditions.

Approach: Developed Python routines to generate and modify G-code, with emphasis on path planning, fiber steering, and experimental repeatability.

Result: Created repeatable toolpath workflows for comparative specimens and research into crack propagation behavior in continuous fiber-reinforced prints.

Tools: Python, G-code generation, composites, continuous fiber 3D printing, experimental testing

CAD • Analysis • DFM

Lightweight Aerospace Structural Bracket

Problem: Reduce bracket mass while maintaining load-bearing reliability for representative aerospace mounting conditions.

Constraints: Factor of safety targets, mounting interfaces, stress concentrations, machining access, and inspection-friendly geometry.

Approach: Used CAD iterations, hand calculations, and FEA assumptions to compare load paths and remove material where structurally appropriate.

Result: Produced a lightweight bracket concept with clear design rationale and manufacturability considerations.

Tools: SolidWorks/Fusion 360, FEA, structural calculations, DFM, factor of safety

Mechanical Design • Automation • CNC Workflow

Automated CNC Part Gripper

Problem: Reduce setup-time and operator-dependent handling steps in a twin-turret CNC lathe workflow.

Constraints: Machine envelope, part access, clamping security, repeatability, chip/coolant exposure, and safe integration with existing operations.

Approach: Developed a mechanical gripper concept focused on reliable part transfer, simple actuation, and practical maintenance.

Result: Defined a manufacturable automation concept intended to improve consistency and reduce cycle interruption.

Tools: Mechanical design, CNC process planning, automation concepts, manufacturability review
