Fiber Toolpaths

Continuous Fiber 3D Printing Toolpath System

Problem: Improve strength and crack resistance in printed composite specimens.

Constraints: Fiber steering limits, nozzle motion continuity, printability, and testable sample geometry.

Approach: Developed Python-based G-code workflows to control fiber path direction and loading response.

Result: Produced repeatable toolpaths for comparative testing and improved control of crack propagation behavior.

Tools: Python, G-code, composite mechanics, experimental testing

PAROL 6 Robotic Arm

PAROL 6 Robotic Arm Build

Problem: Assemble and validate a multi-axis robotic arm as a platform for motion control experiments.

Constraints: Printed-part tolerances, belt alignment, motor wiring, joint access, and reliable serviceability.

Approach: Integrated 3D-printed mechanical parts, motors, belts, fasteners, wiring, and control software while debugging fit-up issues.

Result: Advanced a functional robotic arm system for future controls, calibration, and end-effector development.

Tools: 3D printing, robotics assembly, controls, electromechanical troubleshooting

Custom Macro Pad

Custom Computer Macro Pad

Problem: Create a compact input device for repeatable computer shortcuts and workflows.

Constraints: Switch spacing, USB access, enclosure wall thickness, top-plate fit, and print quality.

Approach: Designed and printed an enclosure around electronics packaging, assembly clearances, and ergonomic desk use.

Aerospace Bracket

Lightweight Aerospace Structural Bracket

Problem: Reduce mass while maintaining load-bearing reliability for aerospace mounting conditions.

Constraints: Factor of safety targets, interface geometry, fastener access, and machinable features.

Approach: Combined CAD iterations, hand calculations, and FEA to optimize geometry and stress flow.

Result: Delivered a weight-optimized concept with documented manufacturability and validation assumptions.

Tools: SolidWorks/Fusion 360, FEA, structural calculations, DFM
