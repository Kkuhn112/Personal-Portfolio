About

An engineer who has actually made the part.

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Colorado Denver who learned engineering on a shop floor before I learned it in a lecture hall. I started in an AS9100 aerospace prototype facility at Lockheed Martin, moved into production CNC machining, and now split my time between undergraduate composites research and teaching.

That path shapes how I design. I think about manufacturing from the first sketch — print orientation, workholding, tool reach, clearances, tolerances, and how a model actually gets made, assembled, and tested. I'm drawn to aerospace, robotics, and defense hardware: problems where precision, weight, and reliability genuinely matter.

This summer I'll be a Production Engineering Intern at True Anomaly, a space and defense company. I'm looking for teams building serious hardware, where an engineer who can design it, machine it, wire it, and test it is worth having in the room.
