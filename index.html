About

Background

I am a mechanical engineering student at the University of Colorado Denver. I began in an AS9100 aerospace prototype shop at Lockheed Martin, moved into production CNC machining, and now divide my time between undergraduate composites research and teaching.

That experience shapes how I design. I account for manufacturing from the first sketch: print orientation, workholding, tool access, clearances, tolerances, and how a model is actually made, assembled, and tested. My focus is aerospace, robotics, and defense hardware, where precision, weight, and reliability carry real consequences.

In the summer of 2026 I will join True Anomaly, a space and defense company, as a production engineering intern. I am interested in teams building serious hardware, where an engineer who can design, machine, wire, and test a part adds value across the process.
