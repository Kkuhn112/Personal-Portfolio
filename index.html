About

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Colorado Denver (GPA 3.92, expected graduation May 2028) with hands-on aerospace manufacturing and research experience. My background includes AS9100 prototype machining at Lockheed Martin, CNC machining at Omni-X, undergraduate research in continuous fiber-reinforced 3D printing, and teaching assistant work supporting engineering programming. I enjoy solving practical design and manufacturing problems through rigorous analysis, iterative prototyping, and clear technical communication.

I also have experience designing parts around real manufacturing constraints for both 3D printing and CNC machining. That includes thinking through print orientation, support strategy, clearances, fastener access, workholding, tool reach, tolerances, and how a CAD model will actually be made, assembled, and tested.

Fiber Toolpaths

Continuous Fiber 3D Printing Toolpath System

Problem: Improve strength and crack resistance in printed composite specimens.

Approach: Developed Python-based G-code workflows to control fiber path direction and loading response.

Result: Produced repeatable toolpaths for comparative testing and improved control of crack propagation behavior.

Tools: Python, G-code, composite mechanics, experimental testing

PAROL 6 Robotic Arm

PAROL 6 Robotic Arm Build

Problem: Build, wire, and tune a multi-axis robotic arm for controlled motion testing.

Approach: Integrated 3D-printed structures, motors, belts, wiring, and control software while troubleshooting mechanical fit-up.

Result: Advanced a functional robotic arm platform for motion experiments and future end-effector work.

Tools: 3D printing, robotics assembly, controls, electromechanical troubleshooting

Custom Macro Pad

Custom Computer Macro Pad

Problem: Create a compact, durable input device for repeatable computer shortcuts.

Approach: Designed a printable enclosure around switch spacing, USB access, assembly clearance, and desk use.

Result: Produced a working macro pad that combines electronics packaging with design for 3D printing.

Tools: CAD, FDM printing, keyboard switches, enclosure design

Aerospace Bracket

Lightweight Aerospace Structural Bracket

Problem: Reduce mass while maintaining load-bearing reliability for aerospace mounting conditions.

Approach: Combined CAD iterations, hand calculations, and FEA to optimize geometry and stress flow.

Result: Delivered a weight-optimized concept with documented manufacturability and validation assumptions.

Tools: SolidWorks/Fusion 360, FEA, structural calculations, DFM
