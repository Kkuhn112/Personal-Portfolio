Continuous Fiber 3D Printing Toolpath System

Problem: Improve strength and crack resistance in printed composite specimens.

Constraints: Fiber steering limits, nozzle motion continuity, and testable sample geometry.

Approach: Developed Python-based G-code workflows to control fiber path direction and loading response.

Result: Produced repeatable toolpaths for comparative testing and improved control of crack propagation behavior.

Lightweight Aerospace Structural Bracket

Problem: Reduce mass while maintaining load-bearing reliability for aerospace mounting conditions.

Constraints: Factor of safety targets, interface geometry, and machinable features.

Approach: Combined CAD iterations, hand calculations, and FEA to optimize geometry and stress flow.

Result: Delivered a weight-optimized concept with documented manufacturability and validation assumptions.

Automated CNC Part Gripper

Problem: Minimize setup and transfer time in twin-turret CNC lathe operations.

Constraints: Limited work envelope, repeatability requirements, and safe integration with existing workflow.
